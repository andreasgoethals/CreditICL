**07_spectrum_by_variant** — `spectrum_by_variant`

**10_adj_correlated_defaults** — `adj_correlated_defaults`

**11_adj_reject_inference** — `adj_reject_inference`

**12_adj_shift_kinds** — `adj_shift_kinds`
