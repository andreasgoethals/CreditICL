  Read the LAST column, not the first. A range can span a real value on

  so 'spans 7/7' is a much weaker statement than it looks.

--- WHAT THIS MEANS ----------------------------------------------------------
The original TabICL prior standard-scales its target, so it puts almost
nothing inside [0,1] and produces boundary atoms only by chance ties at the
+-4 SD outlier clamp. Our prior draws LGD from the zero-and-one inflated beta
regression the LGD literature simulates from (Li, Zhang & Zhao 2020):
the chance of a full recovery (0) and of a total loss (1) and the mean of
the interior beta each depend on the features and on a macro factor shared
by a period's defaults. Each table is its own portfolio: intercepts
normal(-0.54, 1) and normal(-1.46, 1) around the published
-0.54 and -1.46, precision log-uniform on [1, 20] around the published 5.
Nothing is set from the datasets above; they are shown only for comparison.
Training sees the target standardised on the context rows (an affine map:
the atoms stay atoms).

Caveat: this describes the PRIOR, not downstream performance. Whether a
closer-looking prior actually transfers is what the training and evaluation
pipelines measure.
